Four candidate SINE families nobody has described

AnnoSINE_v2 was run on two genomes with no SINE library: Pomacea canaliculata (freshwater snail, Mollusca) and Acanthaster planci (crown-of-thorns starfish, Echinodermata). It proposed two candidates in each. These are the alignments, and the tool's verdict on them.

Unlike everything else in this project there is no answer key — no curated library, no prior annotation, nothing to check against except your eye.

How these were made

Each candidate consensus was searched against its own genome with blastn, overlapping hits collapsed, and two views built: the 100 highest-scoring hits (top100) and a random 100 (rand100) — or everything, where there were fewer than 100. Each locus was extracted with 400 bp of flank and aligned with the consensus first. This is the step that did not exist before: candidate consensus plus genome, straight to alignments.

Two more genomes are running: Acipenser ruthenus (sterlet sturgeon, 1.9 Gb) and Amblyraja radiata (thorny skate, 2.6 Gb) — groups where SINEs are documented in the literature but these assemblies have no repeat library. Hydra vulgaris also still running.
